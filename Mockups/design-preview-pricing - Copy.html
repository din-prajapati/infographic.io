Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about plans, billing, and design usage.

The Free plan provides 3 AI marketing designs every month at zero cost. No credit card is required to sign up. You can generate real-estate creatives, preview formats for Instagram and WhatsApp, and download high-resolution JPG or PNG assets.
An AI Marketing Design is one completely generated, finished marketing creative. Whether it is a luxury villa showcase flyer, an Instagram carousel slide, an open-house invite, or an amenities infographic, each completed output counts as 1 design from your monthly allowance.
An Editable Design opens up the layered Buildographic Canvas Editor for a particular graphic. This allows you to directly customize typography, edit headline copy, swap agent photos, reposition badges, change background lighting, and fine-tune colors rather than just accepting a flat image.
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your Account Settings. When upgrading, your new design allowances become available immediately and billing is pro-rated for the remainder of your billing period.
Absolutely. There are no lock-in contracts for monthly subscriptions. If you cancel, your account remains active with paid features until the end of your prepaid billing term.
When you hit your limit, you can easily top up design bundles or upgrade to the next tier directly from your canvas or usage dashboard. You will never be charged unexpected overage fees without your explicit consent.
Monthly plan allowances reset at the start of each renewal cycle to keep our pricing predictable and affordable. However, all designs created in previous months remain stored in your Design History and can be downloaded anytime.

Yes, our Team plan includes 5 user seats with shared brand kits, property asset libraries, and collaborative design comments. For large regional brokerages with 15+ agents, our Agency or Enterprise packages provide centralized administrative governance and unified invoicing.
