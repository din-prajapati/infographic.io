## Acceptance Criteria

> **Rule:** ACs are generated by `prd-to-roadmap` or `new-story` with type labels already
> assigned from `PROJECT_CONTEXT.yaml ac_coverage`. Your job is to **review** them for
> accuracy (correct file paths, correct behaviour), not to write or label them manually.
> ACs must be file-specific. "Works correctly" is not an AC.
>
> **Type labels** (`[happy-path]`, `[error-path]`, `[security]`, `[session-expiry]`, etc.)
> are read by `orion harden` to check coverage. Do not remove them. If you edit an AC and
> accidentally remove the label, `harden` will auto-restore it.
>
> **AC type library:** happy-path · error-path · security · session-expiry · idempotency ·
> currency-edge · rate-limit · null-input · concurrent · rollback · offline · edge-case
