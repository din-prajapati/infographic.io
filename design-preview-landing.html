COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Buildographic different from standard AI image tools?

Standard AI image generators return flat pixel files where text cannot be edited and frequently hallucinates gibberish. Buildographic is a specialized Real Estate Visual Marketing & Design Studio. It creates complete, multi-format listings (Instagram Stories, Feed Posts, Print Flyers, and Infographics) with 100% editable vector text, shapes, and dynamic agent brand kits.

Can I use my own brokerage logo and agent headshot?

Yes! In your Brand Kit settings, you can upload your headshot, brokerage logo, license numbers, and official brand hex colors. When generating or opening any template, your branding is dynamically stamped into the design.

What export formats are supported?

All plans support high-resolution PNG and JPG downloads formatted for Instagram Stories/Reels (9:16), Square Feeds (1:1), Website Banners (16:9), and MLS flyers. Paid plans also include 300 DPI vector PDF exports for physical open-house print flyers.

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are zero long-term commitments or lock-ins. You can switch between Monthly and Annual billing or cancel your subscription at any time directly inside your account billing dashboard.
